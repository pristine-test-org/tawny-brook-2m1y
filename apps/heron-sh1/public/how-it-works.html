How Heron works

Heron keeps one shared list in view for everyone who walks through the front door, so nobody has to ask what is next.

  1. Someone adds an item to the shared list.
  2. Heron shows the five newest items on the front page.
  3. Finished items get a check mark, and the rest stay in view.

The list refreshes each time the page opens.
